🛟 Help & Support

Extension not blocking content?
  • Make sure the extension is enabled via the power button (top-right)
  • Check the platform toggle is ON for the site you're on
  • Try refreshing the page
Settings not saving?
  • Toggle a setting off and on again
  • Close and reopen the popup
Site not working properly?
  • Try disabling Reclaim for that site and refresh
  • Use the "Report an Issue" form below
Where is my data stored?
  • All data is stored locally on your device
  • Nothing is sent to any server
